# Phase 8 - Product & Enterprise

## Goal
Make Pink commercially deployable for individuals and organizations.

## Deliverables
- multi-tenant isolation;
- RBAC;
- SSO/OIDC/SAML readiness;
- immutable audit events;
- encrypted secrets;
- environments: dev/staging/prod;
- usage/cost controls;
- regional deployment/data governance;
- connector policy management;
- admin console.

## Acceptance criteria
- one tenant cannot access another tenant's data/tools;
- every privileged action is attributable;
- secrets are server-side and rotatable;
- production environments have explicit deployment gates;
- enterprise admins can restrict tools/models/data flows.
